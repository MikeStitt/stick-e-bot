Build a robot in Onshape
========================

A robot in about twenty printed pieces, built from four numbers. Its height is on the plan page.

Start with :doc:`plan`, which is what you are building and why it is in that order. Then
:doc:`before-you-start`, then :doc:`habits`, which is seven things to do on every page. After that
the pages run in order and each one ends with a version you can come back to.

**This guide is being written, and it says where it has got to.** The three pages that come before
the first part are finished, and so are all fourteen part pages — the robot they build is complete
and standing. :doc:`torso`, :doc:`head` and :doc:`assembly` carry a picture of every step. The
eleven pages after them carry their steps and their numbers, and their pictures are being taken a
page at a time.
